Hunter Trap Simulator

Shows if a hunter trap will go off on Warmane (3.3.5a). Set your facing, pick a target and click or drag on the map to move it.

Facing when you cast
Target
How you cast it
The trap lands 2 yd in front of you when standing, and 2 + 8/3 = 4.67 yd when jumping or running forward.
Where the target stands
Straight ahead turns the target onto your facing line, same distance. The other two put it just inside the zone: on your facing line, or at the corner farthest from you.
